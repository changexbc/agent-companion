# Animated-film portrait theme

This optional theme contains 17 AI-generated, transparent PNG portraits of
16 characters from films directed by Hayao Miyazaki. Ponyo has separate human
and goldfish portraits. The default application theme remains `animal`.

The images were generated with Codex's built-in `image_gen` tool and retain their
original 1254 × 1254 RGBA resolution. They are unofficial fan illustrations;
they are not film screenshots or artwork supplied by the film studios.

Character names, films, file names, SHA-256 hashes and generation prompts are
recorded in [cinema-provenance.json](cinema-provenance.json). The record contains
no local machine paths or account information.
